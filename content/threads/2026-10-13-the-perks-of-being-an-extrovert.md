By expressing our thoughts freely, we get to know our fallbacks and get the audience for our work, thereby it kind off motivates us to push much more effort in our art of work. By being able to talk and initiate first and engage often, gives us upper hand in debate/conversation. People starts to admire you for your openness and display of honest/transparency in engaging. At the same time, some tend to envy you and anticipates your downfall. Ignore them at the very best. But one can say the current society is not for introverts because people who express themselves without any fear of criticism and backlash, illuminates/shines brightly. Extroverts beam like a bulb, always being active when around people, forming acquaintances and thereby building fruitful connections and making use of those connections in need. Introverted shut their expressions and be wary of new people and takes time to become acquainted. Becomes shy to ask for favours or doubts when faced. Lack of effective communication will cost us greatly in forming allies, facing risk. Better watch out for limitations in your oral, communication, vocal skills when you are in collaborative environment. Its kind of liberty to speak out of our opinions, suggestions and shortcomings rather than strangling ourselves by hiding them in our hearts. Individuals prefer to collaborate more with folks that are active and expressive in nature.

The guy who confines thoughts, ideas and suggestions to themselves are not favoured in this current fast-paced society. But we should express ourselves and our ideas in brilliant and befitting to business way rather than being too rude to the face. Restrict yourselves from becoming the earlier part. Maintain decency and rationale when surrounded with folks of higher status, refrain from being cold-shouldered. Maintain appreciable levels of interaction among your peers. As a human, forming connections stand at the pinnacle of the civilization. so abstain any ideas of isolation when your collaboration, interaction adds some value to your & theirs's ambitions
