Pain more than often feels uncomforting upon oneself, As a result we resort to escape the pain. But pain can be put to good use. There are many evidence pointing to situations where men risen from the depths of pain, pressure. Pain transcends our minds to achieve greater things. Yeah, it feels suck to feel painful but that pain allows our minds, talents to evolve and strive for riches. It sharpens our resolve to keep doing what we have been doing if we treat pain as a catalyst to our growth. Pain when mistook for stumble, sign of retreat will bring shame upon oneself. There is pain at every major step we took at life. Pain is inevitable no matter you to try to dodge it, it haunts you with in form of regret or guilt. As many would say those achieved greater things in life they feel pain of regret is much worse compared to the pain of facing it squarely. Dealing with the situation, throwing tantrums on us, and still moving forward is a privilege which most lack. As majority terrifies the idea of infliction of pain when dealt with unforeseen circumstances. Pain will draw out our hidden skills and talents that have been inhibited due to no challenges when we embrace pain gracefully. Pain is always uncomfortable to face but we shine, upscale when we are in uncomfortable, pressurized situations more than often. We feel mental stress when we get stuck at a project, Our muscle gets soared at the last reps of the last set, it is traumatic to say goodbyes when it is needed but not we desired, Our minds get tired when planning out a idea, stuck while on the way to our goals, seeing no improvement on our progress, discouraged upon repeated setbacks, these situations will make us feel at the edge of mountain and yet these situations when disregarded as a sign of withdrawal will let us draw out best cards and our creative minds. Imbue the pain, will eventually imbue us from upgrading oneself. Accept the pain as a stepping stone, you will mine a gold field ahead. When we reject yielding to pain, we allows us minds to resort to subconscious to make things happen where pain, pressure are transmuted to driving forces. Accepting the pain vs yielding to pain are two different things. Preparing our minds to embrace the pain, pressure upon inflicted and becoming better than previous at each infliction is the earlier part. Chickening out at the event of facing pain, thinking of ways of dodging and as a result victimized by it, retreats and regrets later this is the later part. at the core the later part will inflict pain of regret, guilty much dangerous than pain of facing it squarely. The pain of discipline is worthier than pain of regret. Dare to inflict the earlier one to unlock new skills, paths to the ambitions. The pain of discipline feels uncomforting initially but soothing upon repetitions but the pain of regret will haunt us forever, the only way to set off this pain is to use this as a stimulating force to think of what lies ahead and taking actions by drawing out effective plans to optimize our life. There is comfort in accepting uncomfortable circumstances and discomfort in accepting comfortable circumstances. So choose your comfort wisely.
