According to the internet Survival of the fittest can be described as "the biological mechanism of natural selection, meaning that organisms best suited to their specific environment are most likely to survive and reproduce", it can simply put as the entities with best traits among a specific community/species tend to survive and excel in their lives as said by Darwin in his "Origin of species" edition book. This principle still applies in modern society in same intensity as in evolution times. the traits that are helpful and useful gets stand out and passed on their successors. analogous as today society where our skills should be upskilled and upgraded and needs freshness when usual gets no recognition. Those who refuse to adapt and upskill gets crushed by those who abide by this principle. Those reject the idea of evolution and this principle gets knocked down when faced with a participant of superior traits in his/her field. Having creative is one thing and constantly adding it more richness, ideas is another great thing. Earlier part always gets humbled when faced with later part. Staying stagnant and ignorant of his shortcomings and too proud of his meagre skills, talents will not survive in the battlefield of the fittest opponents. Acquire new technologies, refine existing skills, add new skills to your wardrobe of talents. Study the moves, actions taken by giants in your chosen field. find what you lack and ways to acquire thereby reaching the same stance of those leading in the court. Don't get carried away of what you possess, always assess what improvements you can make to achieve things in much smoother manner. chances of pushing back are higher when you are off track of upscaling of your abilities and ignorant of your drawbacks. maintain a 1:1 session with your mind and do a detailed feedback of your stack, tools and work on making the shortcomings of yours better than before and best than ever. ONLY those survive who does effective planning and careful assessment of the field they are in so that they can give their best willfully. Doubt, indecision and fear holds back and brings doom upon oneself. gets laid back in the track, outshined by peers. Always be two steps ahead by honing your existing toolset and acquisition of related new skillset. This all translates into reality by forces of discipline, consistency by taking persistent actions with a effective planning and a envisioned future in mind, backed by strong desire enforced by positive thoughts and high-stimuli. Be sportive and accept, practice constructive criticism of your weaknesses and more importantly take goal-driven actions to become THE fittest and master in your Arena, i.e. to survive/thrive (not just live) for longer period of time.
