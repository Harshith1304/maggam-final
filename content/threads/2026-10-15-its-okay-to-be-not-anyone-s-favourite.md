IT hurts when we are not valued or prioritized in certain situations. The respect cant reciprocate in terms of our strength, value sometimes. It feels awkward and unsettling when we got laid back even though we deserve the claps and recognition. But it happens because we are not always everybody's favorite. Due to ego, envy or any rivalry feeling will make us feel that we are non-preferable. But lack of recognition shouldn't be tied to unworthy. A cricketer doesn't excel or play hockey as comfortably as hockey player but that doesn't imply that cricketer is not valuable rather it boils down to the fact that everyone excels and praised in their field not on unusual settings/field. Lack of Praise shouldn't awake any negative destructive thoughts cause certain audience lacks the eye to appreciate talents. So these cases of not getting the praise as in same wavelengths of our efforts, shouldn't at any cost will make us retreat or stagnant at our progress. ON contrary, you should put on more efforts, energies with no hiccups, there comes a day where we will be talk of the town.

Lack of cognizance of our art shouldn't alleviate our vison and at any cost deprive of our joy, enthusiasm to do great things. Even when people criticize, torment your image at the event of you reached great heights then the problem is with people. So best thing is to do avoid caring about those folks and their talks. Pineapple gets trolled for its hard outer shell and its troublesome way of getting rid of it, yet its value and core gets realised upon eating it. Flocks of folks badmouth and laughing out your achievements shouldn't be reciprocated and treated as a setback. There will always be criticizer who will lash even when someone achieve the impossible thing. There will be group who feels relieved on your setbacks and gets pissed off on your endeavors. A section of talk waits for a chance to belittle your recent activities. let them cry harder and their foul be your fuel not barrages to your barrages. There comes a day where a section of these dirty people gets to realize your valour, feels ashamed of oneself. Until then let silence be your weapon and don't care mechanism be your defensive strategy. In order to be second to none burn like sun until You become SUN(Cant be avoided) a.k.a. Burning Star. Feeling down because you are always a option or backup in case of failure, will lower our self-esteem in gatherings, but transmuting those negative solace feelings those inspiring emotions will become a fodder for our subconscious to manifest our comeback. deter yourself from becoming a victim to those feelings. Weaponize those feelings and fuel up your mind tank with extreme stimuli to drive you towards becoming second to none, where people have only 2 options:

1 Making You their favorite

2 Hating you to their core.

Ignore the later ones and cherish the earlier ones. Chase the version of you that you wanted to be, not the version people wanted you to be->(in order to be their favorite). Relish victory but not at the cost of becoming NON-YOU. Individuals behave, assume according to stereotypic images in their minds. Based on those images they form notions and abide acc to them. So you end up disliked by them if you not fit in their idealistic stereotypic images. Due to this dogmatic nature of certain individuals, groups, communities they end up throwing slurs, lashes pointed at you. So refrain them from deciding your outlook, actions and outcomes substituting YOU. Leave them, let them float in their pre-conceived nature. Your endeavors, achieved milestones should do the talking and defending YOU. Don't get cornered by feeling low due to lack of support, applause. You must strike through your life, nonetheless, towards your ambitions.
