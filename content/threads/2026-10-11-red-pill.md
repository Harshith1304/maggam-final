Complicating ourselves by fetching for more motivation, it vanishes upon checking ourselves against reality. motivation behaves like a muscle, willpower behaves like a muscle, analogous to muscle these stimuli wears off, soars, upon usage. You are truly free by not waiting for perfect moment, right amount of motivation. Even if you don't feel it do it anyway cause it wires your subconscious to form it as a habit thereby it becomes a undeniable routine of your life thereafter. FOR your monotonous tasks, it gets done automatically without spending willpower on it, subconsciously. Motivation isn't a bad thing, but it is when used ineffectively. use it only when you want to amplify your output/results. SHOW UP even when you don't have mood for it, mental energies for it. Discipline takes you much forward compared to Motivation. Discipline pushes you to heights consistently wherein willpower, motivation fails to do so but in bursts like canon (falls down after some time). Force you way through usage of willpower when dealt with situation that is unusual in nature and pair it with motivation to force through. but for task that demands consistency and repetency but Resorting to these forces doesn't sound good as they cant give you same kick always and fails to push you as previous or as required.
